🎮 Untitled JavaScript Game

I started this short project back in May of 2025 and worked on during my freetime during school, usually during advisory period. I'm aware that there are still many optimizations and improvements that could be made, but for what it's worth, I'm pretty proud of it.

If you couldn't already tell, this is inspired by Kodiqi's "Idle Breakout" web game, as I thought it'd be fun to experiment with ball-square collision systems and a shop. Keep in mind that progress does not save!


Controls

Use your mouse to click on squares, damaging them by one. Each damage point gives you +$1. Click on ten squares to purchase your first basic ball.

Use number keys 0-9 to alter the game speed. Do note that higher speeds can cause the balls to clip through the indestructible black barriers, but who cares? It's all for fun anyway.
